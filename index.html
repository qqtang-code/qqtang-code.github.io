📝 Biography

- I am a 2nd-year Master student at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Soochow University, under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Juntao Li.

- Before that, I obtained the Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics. During my undergraduate studies, I won more than ten national-level awards in Computer Science Category A competitions recognized by the Ministry of Education, and received honors including Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis and Outstanding Student Pacesetter.

- Currently, my research focuses on Efficient Inference for Large Language Models, including but not limited to KV Cache Optimization, Sparse Attention, and Model Optimization.
